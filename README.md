### SBUS comments
SBUS is a tamil tech enthusiast youtuber. He read and reply comments(mostly asking guidance or a detailed review of the product they have been using) of his subscribers and members of the channel in videos but as there are almost more than 2000+ videos uploaded in the channel, and would increase day by day. It became impossible to find when and where SBUS has replied to the comment/query/guidance asked by the user. This project aims to solve this problem. Channel Link - [link](https://www.youtube.com/@Sivabharani)

## Method: commenter readout by prompting
SBUS reads a commenter's name/handle aloud right before reading their comment, in a small set of cue patterns found by watching videos and verified against all 1.18M chunks:

- `name + ப்ரோ`, bare `name`, `name + என்ன சொல்லிருக்காரு/சொல்லிருக்காங்க/சொல்லிருக்கான்`, `name + வந்திருக்காது`, `name + அப்படிங்கற/அப்படிங்கிற நண்பர் வந்து`, `name + என்ன கேட்டுருந்தாரு`, compound English usernames in Tamil script (`டார்க் ஹன்டர்` -> `darkhunter`)
- the cue word is `நண்பர்` (ண); `நம்பர்` (ம) means phone number and is never a handle
- spoken English usernames are captioned in Tamil script, and `சொல்லிருக்காரு`-type cues are written fused (no spaces), so the prompt lists transcript spellings, not spoken spellings

The canonical prompt is `prompt-templates.md`. Each notebook embeds its own copy; `build_pilot_v5_nb.py` asserts at build time that the embedded prompt matches the template.
